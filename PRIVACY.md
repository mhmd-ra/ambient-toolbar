# Privacy Policy — Ambient Toolbar for YouTube

This add-on does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- Video colors are read locally, inside your browser, only to tint your toolbar. Frames are reduced to a handful of colors and are never saved or sent anywhere.
- Your settings (brightness, saturation, etc.) are stored locally with Firefox's `storage` API and never leave your device.
- If you import a settings file, it is read locally from your device and only its setting values are kept.
- The add-on makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.
