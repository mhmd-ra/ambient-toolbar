Privacy Policy

Ambient Toolbar for YouTube · Last updated October 14, 2026

This add-on does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • Video colors are read locally, inside your browser, only to tint your toolbar. Each frame is reduced to a handful of colors that are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings are stored locally with Firefox's storage API and never leave your device.
  • If you import a settings file, it is read locally from your device and only its setting values are kept.
  • The add-on makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking or ads.

Permissions

  • theme: to change the colors of the tab bar and toolbar.
  • storage: to remember your settings.
  • Access to youtube.com: to read the colors of the video you're watching.
